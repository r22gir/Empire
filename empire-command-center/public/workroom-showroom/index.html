From your vision to the finished room.

  1. 01You send the vision — photos, measures, fabric or finish notes, and the look you want.
  2. 02We send a clear quote, with idea drawings when they help the piece read. Those sheets show the idea. They are not a design of the whole project, and they are not construction documents.
  3. 03The shop makes it — sewing room, frame shop, or both.
  4. 04We finish it in the room, ready for your client.
