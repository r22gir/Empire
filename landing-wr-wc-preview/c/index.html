Empire Workroom is the soft-goods shop for designers and trade. You envision the room. We produce and finish the drapery, upholstery, and seating so that vision is the one in the house. With the quote we can include idea drawings — sheets of the piece — so the idea is plain before it is made. Your design stays yours. WoodCraft joins when the job needs furniture or millwork, on the same shop floor.

The designer holds the vision. We care about the handwork that lands it: pleat, welt, seat, the fall of the cloth. Talk to us about the room, the fabric, and the date.

How we work with you

From your vision to the finished room

01You send the vision — photos, measures, fabric or finish notes, and the look you want.
02A clear quote, with idea drawings when they help the piece read. Those sheets show the idea. They are not a design of the whole project, and they are not construction documents.
03The shop makes it — sewing room, frame shop, or both.
04We finish it in the room, ready for your client.
How jobs stay organized on our side

EmpireBox is the back office for photos, pricing, and drawings across Workroom and WoodCraft. Most designers never open it. People and craft do the work; the software keeps a job from living in five email threads.
