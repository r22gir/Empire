For designers & trade

Your designs come true

Drapery, Romans, banquettes, beds, and soft seating — the rooms and cloth you picture, made so the beauty is real.

Empire Workroom is the soft-goods shop for designers and trade. You envision the room. We produce and finish the drapery, upholstery, and seating so that vision is the one in the house. With the quote we can include idea drawings — sheets of the piece — so the idea is plain before it is made. Your design stays yours. WoodCraft joins when the job needs furniture or millwork, on the same shop floor.

From your vision to the finished room

You design. We produce and finish. Idea sheets, when they help, travel with the quote.

  1. 01You send the vision — photos, measures, fabric or finish notes, and the look you want.
  2. 02We send a clear quote, with idea drawings when they help the piece read. Those sheets show the idea. They are not a design of the whole project, and they are not construction documents.
  3. 03The shop makes it — sewing room, frame shop, or both.
  4. 04We finish it in the room, ready for your client.
How jobs stay organized on our side

If you ever want to know how we track photos, pricing, and drawings across Workroom and WoodCraft, that back-office layer is EmpireBox. Most designers never need to open it. It is how we keep a job from living in five email threads: intake and measures, pricing, and day-to-day ops. It is not a designer tool, and it is not the work on this page.
