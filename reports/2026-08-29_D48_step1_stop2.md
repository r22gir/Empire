## §5 · STOP 2 findings

1. **FK enforcement is declared, not applied** — schema has the constraint; uvicorn-running empire-backend does not enable FKs on its connection. D50 routes the 74 call sites through the factory; STEP 2 hardens writers to produce valid customer_id. Until both happen, the constraint is a statement of intent.

2. **`invoices.quote_id` has 19 broken references** — already a finding from STEP 0, confirmed in rebuild preconditions check. Surviving as plain TEXT. A future dispatch needs to either recreate fixture quotes or accept lossy-NULL on those 19 rows.

3. **`jobs.invoice_id` has 4 broken references** — same shape as above, smaller scale. Ruling C kept this unenforced.

4. **`payments_v2` is empty** — still the aggregate pitfall from STEP 0: 6 queries (`financial_service.py:156/167/177/244/315`, `lifecycle_service.py:502`, `payments.py:354`) read `payments_v2.amount` and return $0 silently. The 1 real payment (INV-2026-002, $100 cash) is invisible to them. Founder ruled this out of scope for D48; **logged as new H-number `H78`** for tracking.

5. **168 non-chain FK violations** — `cf_*` (140) + `ag_archives` (19) + `openclaw_tasks` (8) + `lf_activities` (1). Per ruling D, rows left untouched, `cf_*` unaudited. **They will silently surface when global FK enforcement is enabled in D50**, since the 168 are real existing-row violations.

6. **lifecycle_service.py:285 reads `payments_v2`** (empty) instead of `payments` (1 row) — bug surfaced in STEP 0, unchanged.

7. **`customers.type` CHECK constraint disallows 'legacy'** — the recreated fixture customer had to use default `'residential'`. Identification is via name + notes + `source='d48_recreate'`, not type. Worth flagging if any future dispatch wants to mark legacy customers by type.
