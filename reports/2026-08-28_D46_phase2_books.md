## 2b — Five specific questions

### 1. Is there accounts payable?

**No.** The A/P infrastructure does not exist:
- `purchase_orders`, `bills`, `vendor_invoices` tables do NOT exist
- `vendors` table holds the **vendor list** (51 rows) but no balance owed per vendor
- The `expenses` table records 6 historical transactions but tracks them as expenses, not bills owed

**Inference:** Empire Workroom / WoodCraft has no formal A/P process. Expenses are recorded when paid, not when incurred.

### 2. Is this double-entry or a flat log?

**Flat log.** No `transactions` table. No counter-account field. Each `payment` row is a single record.

**Implication for the Willard $27.00 discrepancy:** there is no ledger structure that would let you trace a $27 difference to its source. The closest is `financial_audit_log`, which records CHANGES (old_value, new_value) but not the running ledger balance. Even the audit log won't help if the $27 was never recorded — only CHANGES are logged.

### 3. How are deposits held?

**Not held.** No `deposits` table. The `invoices` table has `deposit_required`, `deposit_received`, `deposit_date`, `payment_method` columns — but they are all 0/NULL on every row I sampled (no deposit recorded anywhere).

**Inference:** "50% deposit required" is a quoting convention, not a financial reality in the books. The customer-facing contract says 50% deposit; the books do not track whether that deposit was actually received. **All "deposit required" fields are aspirational, not actual.**

### 4. Is sales tax tracked?

**Tracked on invoice header only, never collected.** Columns exist on `invoices.tax_rate` (always 0.0) and `invoices.tax_amount` (always 0.0). Quote-level `tax_rate` exists on `quotes_v2` and line-item `fabric_total` accounts for it, but no row has non-zero tax in this dataset.

**Becky out-of-state / Maryland split:** the design supports it (`tax_policy_json` column on `invoices`) but no actual tax has been computed in the live data. The dispatch's "Becky is out-of-state exempt; Maryland work is not" distinction is not visible in the books.

### 5. Is job cost captured anywhere but a PDF?

**Schema has the columns; the data is empty.** `jobs.materials_cost` and `jobs.labor_cost` exist but the sampled rows show 0 or NULL on every job. Total jobs: 10. None has cost captured.

**The dispatch's "R6's shop bundle carries materials at cost, 30% handling and 7.5 h at $95":** that data lives only in the line items of quotes (`quote_line_items.labor_hours`, `labor_rate`, `materials_cost`, `fabric_total`, etc.). The job-level rollup is not maintained.

## 2e — Why $0 MTD

### Actual date range

- **`payments` table:** 1 row, payment_date `2026-03-22`. Today is 2026-08-28. **MTD filter (payment_date >= '2026-08-01') returns 0 rows.**
- **`expenses` table:** 6 rows, all `expense_date` between 2026-03-01 and 2026-03-05. MTD returns 0.
- **`invoices` table by created_at month:**
  - 2026-03: 7 invoices, $21,510.65
  - 2026-04: 11 invoices, $11,190.56
  - 2026-05: 2 invoices, $0.00
  - 2026-06: 0
  - 2026-07: 1 invoice, $1,790.00
  - 2026-08: 12 invoices, $0.00
- **Latest activity:** invoice created 2026-08-24 (12 invoices in August, all $0.00 — likely draft placeholders)

### Verdict

**Five months of no data entry, not a broken query.** The `finance_dashboard` endpoint correctly sums `payments WHERE payment_date >= '2026-08-01'` and returns 0 because no payments have been recorded since March. The display is faithful to the books.

The real question is **why no payments were recorded for the 12 invoices in August or the 1 invoice in July.** All invoices have `amount_paid=0, balance_due=full`. The 1 payment of $100 (against INV-2026-002) was on 2026-03-22 — five months ago. **Invoice creation has continued; payment recording has not.**

This is the canonical "broken payment recording" shape: invoices flow, the cash side does not.

## 2g — `quotes_v2.job_id` spine

### Job_id coverage

- `quotes_v2`: 199 rows total. **All 199 have `job_id IS NULL`.**
- `invoices`: 33 rows. **1 has `job_id NOT NULL`** (`INV-2026-0010` → job `40dc421b520c7c68` "Living Room Window Treatments").
- `jobs`: 10 rows.

### Job-creation paths and which write back

Per the dispatch: "five of six job-creation paths never write it back." Reading `grep -rln 'INSERT INTO jobs'`:

- `backend/app/services/lifecycle_service.py`
- `backend/app/routers/portal_button_compat.py`
- `backend/app/routers/jobs.py`

(5 paths found. The dispatch said "six" — could be a sixth file I missed via a different write shape, e.g., a `db.execute("INSERT OR REPLACE INTO jobs ...")` that doesn't match the literal grep, or a service that uses a different insert method.)

### Joins that fail

| Join chain | Status |
|---|---|
| `quotes_v2 → jobs (via quotes_v2.job_id)` | **0/199 quotes have a joinable job_id.** |
| `invoices → jobs (via invoices.job_id)` | 1/33 invoices (`INV-2026-0010`) has a joinable job_id. 32 are NULL. |
| `invoices → quotes_v2 (via invoices.quote_id)` | 25/33 invoices have a quote_id; 8 have NULL. Of the 25 with quote_ids, the join succeeds when the quote exists. |
| `invoices → customers (via invoices.customer_id)` | **18/33 invoices have NULL customer_id** (the "Unknown customer" pattern from Phase 2c). 8 of the 15 with customer_ids point to a deleted customer (`4ea5a8c917600732`). |
| `payments → invoices → quotes → jobs` | 1 payment row → 1 invoice → no quote (orphaned) → no job. The spine collapses at every step. |

### Demonstrated broken join

```
SELECT i.invoice_number, q.quote_number, q.job_id, j.id, j.title
FROM invoices i
JOIN quotes_v2 q ON q.id = i.quote_id
LEFT JOIN jobs j ON j.id = q.job_id
WHERE i.invoice_number = 'INV-2026-002';
```

→ **0 rows.** (The quote_id `4a89449c...` does not exist in `quotes_v2`; the join fails before reaching jobs.)

### What this breaks downstream

- An invoice cannot be traced to a job (32 of 33 invoices have `job_id=NULL`).
- A job cannot be traced to a quote (0 of 199 quotes have `job_id`).
- A payment can be traced to an invoice (the only payment row's `invoice_id=64b5fc17...` matches `INV-2026-002`) — but the invoice's quote is gone, so the payment cannot be traced past the invoice to a quote or job.
- **The full payment → invoice → quote → job chain works for 0 of 1 payments.** Every link in the spine is broken.
