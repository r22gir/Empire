### Two structural findings

1. **Trust vs Smart mode.** Six writers use trust mode (take `customer_id` as given — NULL propagates). Eight writers use smart mode (find-or-create customer from denormalized email/name). The trust-mode writers are the ones that can produce chain-violating rows today.
2. **`lifecycle_service.create_job_from_quote` is the only path that writes `quotes_v2.job_id` back** — but it operates in trust mode.
