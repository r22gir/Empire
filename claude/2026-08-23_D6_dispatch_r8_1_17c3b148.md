## NOTES FOR THE FOUNDER

- **Step 5 is where I'd put the money.** `openclaw-deepseek.env` exists and is
  loaded by the service, so a real provider is configured. If the resolution
  path never reads those variables, the answer to "why is model=openclaw" is
  that resolution silently no-ops and the service name is what's left in the
  field. That would make it a small fix, not a rebuild.

  `(provider=…, model=…)` suffix entirely, the two populations were produced
  by different code and R7's "one root cause, 95.5%" figure is an artifact of
  normalising the digits and quotes out of the strings before grouping them.
  That normalisation is exactly what would merge two different errors that
  share a first sentence.
