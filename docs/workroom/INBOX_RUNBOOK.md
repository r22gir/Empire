## Same-day triage

1. Log every new designer email or DM the same business day on the [manual capture page](/workroom/capture). One inbound = one capture. Use the LeadForge field set. Leave unknown fields blank. 2. Reply yourself from `empirebox2026@gmail.com` (the workroom alias) the same day, even when the price is not ready. Ask for piece and job type, measures, fabric or COM, finish, location, photos, and install date.
3. After you reply, set `last_contacted_at` and `next_action` on the capture row. That button records the fact. It does not send the reply.
4. Set status to `new`, `needs_info`, `quoting`, `quoted`, `won`, `lost`, or `hold` before you leave the row.
